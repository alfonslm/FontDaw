# Installing FFmpeg

Exporting a score as a video needs the FFmpeg program, which FontDaw does not include.

## What FFmpeg is for, and how FontDaw finds it

FFmpeg is a separate, free program that turns the score's pictures and sound into a video file. FontDaw does not include it, and needs its libraries on your computer. You only need it to export a score as a video; audio export and everything else work without it.

When you choose a video format in the export dialog and FFmpeg is missing, the page tells you so. It offers two buttons:

- **Download FFmpeg**, which points you to download information. (This button still opens a MuseScore page today; FontDaw's own page will replace it.)
- **Locate existing installation**, which lets you choose the folder where FFmpeg already is.

You can set the same folder at any time in **Preferences > Video**.
