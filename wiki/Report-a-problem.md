# Report a problem

How to tell us about a bug.

## Before you report

Open **About FontDaw**. It shows the version, in the form `0.1.0:` followed by a build number, and a **Revision** line with a copy button. The button copies your operating system, processor type, the program name and version, and the revision to the clipboard. Paste that into your report.

Also tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. If you can, attach a small project that shows the problem.

FontDaw keeps its settings and documents in folders of its own, separate from MuseScore Studio's.
