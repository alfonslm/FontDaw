# Privacy

FontDaw does not collect data about you.

## Network connections

FontDaw makes no network connections of its own. It does not check for updates, send crash reports, sign you in anywhere or upload anything. We checked this once, on Linux in November 2026, by logging all connections while starting the program, playing, editing and saving a score. There were none. We have not checked on Windows or macOS, and we will check again before each release.

Some menu items and messages link to web pages. Those open in your web browser only when you click them, and then your browser's privacy rules apply, not FontDaw's.

If you add a plug-in, that plug-in may do its own network access. FontDaw cannot control that.
