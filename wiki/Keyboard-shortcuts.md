# Keyboard shortcuts

These are the shortcuts FontDaw has today. They are the ones inherited from MuseScore Studio. FontDaw is planned to get a shortcut layout of its own, next to a MuseScore layout for people who prefer it. Nothing more is decided yet.

## Playback

- **Space:** play.
- **Shift+Space:** play from the selection.
- **Ctrl+Space:** pause and select the playback position (**Alt+Space** on the Mac set).

Stop, rewind, loop and metronome have no key by default. You can give them one in **Preferences > Shortcuts**.

## Note input

- **N:** note input by note name. **M:** note input by duration.
- **A** to **G:** enter that note (B also on **H**).
- **0:** rest. **+** and **-:** sharp and flat. **T:** tie.
- Number keys set the note length: **1** is the shortest (64th), **5** a quarter, **7** a whole note.
- **Q** halves and **W** doubles the duration.
- **Ctrl+Up** and **Ctrl+Down:** an octave up or down. **Alt+Up** and **Alt+Down:** move to the chord above or below.

## Changing shortcuts

Open **Preferences > Shortcuts**. FontDaw ships shortcut sets for standard, Mac and French (AZERTY) keyboards. There is no keyboard-layout setting yet.
