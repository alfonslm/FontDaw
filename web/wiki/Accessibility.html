Accessibility

What FontDaw does for people who use a screen reader, the keyboard only, or need high contrast.

What exists

FontDaw is based on MuseScore Studio and inherits its accessibility features. We have not tested them in FontDaw yet, so we cannot say how well they work.

  • High contrast. On first launch you can pick a high-contrast theme, white or black, and switch on high contrast. More high-contrast settings are in Preferences.
  • Keyboard. The shortcuts come in sets for standard, French (AZERTY) and Mac keyboards.
  • Screen readers. Notes and other score items tell screen readers about themselves, and there is braille support. How complete this is has not been checked.

The new audio features, such as audio tracks, have not been checked for accessibility at all.
