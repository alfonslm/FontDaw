Plug-ins and sounds

Where the sound of a score comes from.

SoundFonts

FontDaw plays SoundFont files (.sf2 and .sf3) with a built-in synthesiser, so you hear your score without installing anything else. You can choose a SoundFont for an instrument from the sound menu of its mixer channel. A Playback setup dialog holds sound profiles, which you can activate or set as the default for new scores.

VST3 instruments and effects

FontDaw can host VST3 plug-ins, both instruments and effects. This is inherited from MuseScore Studio and not yet tested in FontDaw. Plug-ins are scanned in a way that is meant to survive a plug-in that crashes, so one bad plug-in should not close the program. We have not yet tested this in a FontDaw build.

Not supported yet

CLAP, LV2 and Audio Unit plug-ins, SFZ sample libraries, a browser for plug-in presets, and built-in synths. FontDaw does not use Muse Sounds.
