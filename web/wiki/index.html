FontDaw

FontDaw is a music workstation built around the score. You write music in standard notation, and the plan is to record, arrange and mix audio around it in the same project.

What FontDaw is

FontDaw is free software, based on MuseScore Studio. It keeps MuseScore's notation editor, parts, printing and mixer. The goal is to add audio tracks, recording and a timeline around the score, so the score stays at the centre of the project instead of being one track among many.

Where things stand

FontDaw is in early development. The version is 0.1.0 and there is nothing to download yet.

Today you can write and print scores as in MuseScore Studio, and you can import an audio file (WAV, FLAC, MP3 or Ogg) that shows up in the score as an audio staff and plays in time with it. Recording, a timeline view and most audio editing are not there yet. Status and roadmap has the details.
